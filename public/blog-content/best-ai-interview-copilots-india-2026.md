## JavihAI

**Best for:** Indian candidates who want India-specific context (salary in LPA, notice period, bond clauses, 6+ Indian languages) and a recurring monthly plan for an active job search.

- **Free:** 10 AI answers/day, every day, no expiry, no card required.
- **Quick Pass — ₹225** (1 hour, one-time, 24-hour window): cheapest option for a single interview tomorrow.
- **Pro — ₹900** (7 days, unlimited, one-time): resume analysis + company-specific context added.
- **Power — ₹1,800/month** (unlimited, auto-renewing subscription): adds AI mock interviews with scoring, evaluation, performance analytics, and priority support.

**Where it wins:** the only one of the two with a genuinely India-tuned mode, a forever-free daily tier, and mock-interview scoring baked into the top plan.

**Where it doesn't:** no one-time "30 days unlimited, no renewal" option — the closest equivalent (Pro) is capped at 7 days, so a full month of unlimited use means committing to the recurring Power subscription.

## InterviewAssist

**Best for:** candidates who want to pay once for a month (or a year) of unlimited access without an auto-renewing subscription, or who prefer metered minutes that never expire.

- **Free:** 10 sessions of 10 minutes each (100 minutes total, then the free tier is used up — it doesn't reset daily).
- **Credit packs** (minutes never expire): Starter ₹299/120 min, Pro ₹499/240 min, Max ₹899/480 min.
- **Unlimited Access, one-time:** ₹1,599 for 30 days (no auto-renewal), or ₹5,999 for 365 days (≈₹500/month effective).

**Where it wins:** its unlimited monthly option (₹1,599, one-time) is actually *cheaper* than JavihAI's recurring Power plan (₹1,800/month) for a single month, and it doesn't auto-renew — you only pay again if you choose to. Credit-pack minutes also never expire, unlike JavihAI's Quick Pass (24-hour window) or Pro (7-day window).

**Where it doesn't:** no India-specific language/salary mode, no AI mock-interview scoring, and its free tier is a fixed 100-minute allowance rather than a daily reset.
