# Pricing — JavihAI

> Machine-readable pricing reference for AI agents and comparison tools. All prices in INR, current as of the date below. For the live page (with any active promotional codes) see https://javihai.in/pricing.

Last updated: 2026-09-25

## Free
- Price: ₹0 / forever
- Billing: none required, no credit card
- Limits: 15 AI answers/day (5 screenshot solves, 5 system-audio answers, 5 mic answers), resets daily at 12:00 AM IST
- Features: full test session (mic, audio, coding copilot), no expiry

## Quick Pass
- Price: ₹349 one-time (list price ₹699; site frequently runs promotional discount codes)
- Billing: one-time, no subscription, no auto-renewal
- Validity: 24 hours from purchase, fully unlimited during that window
- Features: full AI Interview Assistant, Voice Mode, Screen Mode, Coding Interview Support, HR Interview Support
- Best for: a single upcoming interview

## Pro
- Price: ₹1,299 one-time (list price ₹2,999)
- Billing: one-time, no subscription
- Validity: 7 days from purchase, fully unlimited during that window
- Features: everything in Quick Pass, plus Resume Analysis and company-specific interview support
- Best for: an active interview week with multiple rounds

## Power
- Price: ₹2,499/month (list price ₹5,999)
- Billing: monthly subscription, cancel anytime
- Validity: unlimited for the duration of the subscription
- Features: everything in Pro, plus AI Mock Interview, AI Interview Evaluation & Score, Performance Analytics, Personalized Improvement Plan, Priority Support, Early Access Features
- Best for: an ongoing job search

## Common to all paid plans
- No hourly or minute meters — sessions never cut off mid-interview
- No overtime/surcharge fees
- Payment: UPI, Razorpay, credit/debit cards, net banking (India-focused)
- 7-day money-back guarantee on first paid purchase, refunded directly to UPI
- Platforms: Windows 10/11 and macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel)
